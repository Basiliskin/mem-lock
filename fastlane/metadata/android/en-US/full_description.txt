Does your mother or father sometimes leave a bag or phone charger behind? LeaveCheck reminds them to check their belongings when they leave a place.

You set it up once on their phone. After that, there is nothing to start or press. When they have stayed somewhere and walk away, the phone gives a Reminder and repeats it a few times until they tap it.

WHAT IT DOES
• Gives a Reminder after leaving a place where they stayed for a while
• Lets you mark places that always get a Reminder, with your own message
• Lets you mark Quiet Places, such as home, where it stays silent
• Plays your own text or recorded voice, with sound and vibration
• "I forgot something" shows the last place, and opens it in the phone's map app
• Warns when something stops it working, such as location being off or a low battery
• Protects settings from accidental changes

WHAT IT CAN'T DO
• It can't tell whether a bag was left behind. It reminds them to check, every time.
• It can't detect a phone left inside a forgotten bag. The phone needs to be on their body, in a pocket or around the neck.
• It can't guarantee that nothing will be lost.
• Reminders can come late or be missed if the phone restricts background work or location is weak.

YOUR PRIVACY
LeaveCheck uses location all the time, including in the background, to notice when they leave a place. Location stays on the phone. There is no account and no server. It is shared only when they choose to open directions in the map app.

Available in Russian and English. Free.
