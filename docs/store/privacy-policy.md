# LeaveCheck privacy policy (draft)

**Publish this at a public web address before submitting; Play requires one. Add a contact email and the date first.** Developer: [name]. Contact: [email]. Effective: [date].

LeaveCheck reminds a person to check their belongings when they leave a place.

**Location.** The app reads the phone's location all the time, including when closed or not in use, to notice when the person leaves a place. Saved places and the last places visited are stored only on the phone.

**Physical activity.** The app reads the phone's activity signal, such as walking, to notice movement. It is not stored as history.

**Microphone.** Used only when the Helper records a voice Reminder. The recording stays on the phone.

**Sharing.** We have no server or accounts, and collect nothing. Location leaves the phone only when the person opens directions, which sends the destination to the phone's map app under that app's own policy.

**Deleting.** Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone.
